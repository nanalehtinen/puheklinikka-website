## How the uncertain terms were settled (looked up 2026-10-14)

1. **kognitiivinen lyhytterapeutti → "cognitive brief therapist".** "Brief therapy" is the usual English term for this kind of time-limited therapy. Integrum, the Finnish training institute, defines it as therapy of under 20 sessions or a few months ([integrum.fi](https://www.integrum.fi/klinikka/)). I found no official English title, so this is my best rendering.
2. **työnohjaaja → "work supervisor".** The Finnish supervisors' association lists its members in English as "Work Supervisor (STOry)" ([suomentyonohjaajat.fi/en](https://www.suomentyonohjaajat.fi/en/)). "Clinical supervisor" means something narrower in English.
3. **erikoispuheterapeutti → "specialist speech therapist".** I found no official English title. "FL" is "Licentiate of Philosophy (Lic.Phil.)", per the Finnish libraries' Ask a Librarian service ([kirjastot.fi](https://www.kirjastot.fi/kysy/naennaisen-yksinkertaiset-asiat-voivat-joskus?language_content_entity=fi)).
4. **Sosiaali- ja terveysalan tietoturvakortti.** No English name exists, so the Finnish name stays with an English explanation in brackets.
5. **PÄTEVÄ 2.** Työterveyslaitos (the Finnish Institute of Occupational Health) calls the course "Työterveyshuoltoon pätevöittävä koulutus asiantuntijoille" and has no English page ([ttl.fi curriculum](https://www.ttl.fi/sites/default/files/2022-06/Opetussuunnitelma_tth_muut%20asiantuntijat_2op_2022_1.pdf)). The Finnish name stays with an English explanation in brackets.
6. **Luki-testaus ja kuntoutus → "Assessment and rehabilitation of reading and writing difficulties (dyslexia)".** Finnish research uses "lukivaikeus" as a synonym for dyslexia ([jyx.jyu.fi](https://jyx.jyu.fi/handle/123456789/80950)).
7. **Marjaana's "Skillful interaction" link.** It still opens the Finnish page. I don't know whether an English version exists.
8. **Title under the photo** follows the page language (Nana 2026-10-14): "puheterapeutti" on Finnish pages, "talterapeut" on Swedish pages, "Speech therapist" on English pages. The body text uses "speech and language therapist", as on the English Our services page.
9. **Standard English terms used as is:** trauma-informed, selective eating, subacute, AAC.

These sources were read through a search tool, not opened directly.

## Annemari Hongell

**Card:** Speech therapist (Turku) / Cognitive brief therapist / 040 779 6733 / email

Annemari works with adult clients with neurological conditions, providing both individual and group speech therapy. She also offers guidance, therapy and training for voice disorders and voice problems, and helps with difficulties in swallowing and eating. Annemari graduated as a speech and language therapist from Åbo Akademi University in 2015. In her master's thesis, she collected Swedish-language normative data for the assessment tool Test i Snabb Seriell Benämning (Rapid Serial Naming Test).

Alongside her work as a speech and language therapist, Annemari offers [support services for the family members of rehabilitation clients in the form of cognitive brief therapy](../../../palvelut-lheisille/).

**Further training:**
Adult neurological speech therapy: language-cognitive communication difficulties, cerebrovascular disorders, brain injuries, progressive neurological diseases, swallowing difficulties, neurogenic voice and fluency difficulties, functional voice therapy.

**Selected training and qualifications:**
The McNeill Dysphagia Therapy Program Certification, SPEAK OUT!®: A Practical Approach to Treating Parkinson’s, Supported Conversation for Adults with Aphasia (SCA™), LSVT LOUD Training and Certification, Better Conversations with Aphasia (BCA): Conversation Therapy for People with Aphasia and their Families, A Three-Part Treatment Plan for Oral Placement Therapy (OPT™ I), Deep Pharyngeal Neuromuscular Stimulation (DPNS & E-Stim Certification), Goal Attainment Scale (GAS), International Classification of Functioning, Disability and Health (ICF). Annemari is also trained as a cognitive brief therapist and uses this expertise with both rehabilitation clients and their family members.

**Client groups:**
- Young people, working-age adults and older adults
  - Individual and group rehabilitation
  - Subacute and maintenance rehabilitation
  - Speech therapy assessment periods
  - Guidance-focused speech therapy periods
- Cerebrovascular disorders: aphasia, dysarthria, dysphagia (swallowing difficulty), language-cognitive communication difficulties
- Progressive neurological diseases: Parkinson’s disease, ALS, MS, memory disorders
- Traumatic brain injuries and their after-effects
- Neurogenic voice and fluency difficulties
- Functional voice disorders, voice care, vocal ergonomics
- Assessment and rehabilitation of reading and writing difficulties (dyslexia)

Working languages: Finnish, Swedish, English.

## Nana Lehtinen

**Card:** Speech therapist, [Sanapsis](http://www.sanapsis.com) lead / Administrative matters / 040 593 0015 (text messages) / email

Nana has been an entrepreneur at Puheklinikka since 2006. Her responsibilities include developing Puheklinikka’s operations, supervising therapy work and coordinating administrative matters.

Nana graduated as a speech and language therapist from the University of Oulu in 2004. In her master’s thesis, she studied how spoken Finnish changes under the influence of a foreign language. In her doctoral thesis, Nana studied how healthy bilingual people perform in verbal fluency tasks in Finnish and English.

Nana also develops the Sanapsis family of speech therapy apps, designed and built at Puheklinikka.

The Sanapsis family includes three apps:
- SanapsisPro, a trilingual professional app designed for speech and language therapists (Finnish, Swedish and English).
- SanapsisLite, which offers a short preview of the SanapsisPro app.
- Sanapsis+, designed to support clients’ independent word-level practice at home. In addition to exercises, Sanapsis+ offers tips and ideas for activating speech and language functions at home.

Working languages: Finnish, English.
